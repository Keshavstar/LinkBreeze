# LinkBreeze

The self-hosted Linktree alternative you actually own.

One public page holds all your links, backed by a real admin dashboard: privacy-first analytics, QR codes, 11 built-in themes with a full customizer, link scheduling, and a migration wizard that imports from Linktree, Bento, Hopp.bio, and more in about 30 seconds.

## How it works

- Single container, SQLite included: no external database to manage
- The public page is fully server rendered with zero client JavaScript
- On first open, the setup wizard creates your admin account in under a minute
- Manage everything from the dashboard at the app's URL; share your public page at `http://<host>:<port>/<slug>`
- To make the page public, expose the app through a reverse proxy or bind a custom domain (built into the app)
